## 2. Instruction documents (.docx)

### Shared rules, repeated in every doc
- **Canonical records:** the portal is a view over the canonical FirmVora modules (Documents, Intake Forms, Appointments, Messages/Communications, Invoices & Payments, Services, Tax Returns, Clients). It never creates duplicate records.
- **Isolation:** client and tenant isolation, with no client switching. Authorization is checked server-side on every read, write, download, upload, message, reschedule, and payment, not just by hiding buttons.
- **Hidden from clients:** internal staff notes, reviewer comments, internal folders and tasks, admin controls, and unshared documents never appear.
- **Branding:** the tenant's logo and identity, with FirmVora as platform attribution only.
- **Footer:** the simplified footer (tenant logo, Powered by FirmVora, Terms of Service, Conditions). Do not use the old marketing footer.
- **States:** loading uses skeletons that never flash another client's data. Every doc lists empty, error, and permission-denied states, and permission-denied must not leak a record's existence.
- **Audit logging:** uploads, downloads, requests, reschedules, message sends, external-resource opens, and profile or security changes.
- **Upload Documents decision pop-up** (every page):
  - Choice 1: tax documents for my tax preparer. Allowed only with an active tax service.
  - Choice 2: documents for my business services. Allowed only with an active business service (bookkeeping, payroll, formation, advisory…).
  - Choice 3: "I don't have an open service" means **STOP**: no file picker, and an explanation that this is not storage. A client with no eligible service gets STOP even through another button.
  - If several services are eligible, the client chooses, or the service is derived from the request.
  - Uploads started from a request attach to that request.
  - A successful upload is saved with tenant, client, service, category, period/year, uploader, and timestamp.
  - Failures never show as uploaded.
- **Appointment card:**
  - **View Details** opens a modal with date, start time, time zone, staff, service type, meeting method, location or link, status, and instructions.
  - **Reschedule** preselects the existing appointment, shows only firm-allowed availability, updates the same record, keeps history, and sends a confirmation.
  - Empty state: **Schedule Now** if self-scheduling is enabled.
- **Send a Message:** every entry point opens one composer with the client preselected and can link the related record.
- **Recent Activity:** client-visible events only, each deep-linked to its record.
- **Quick Links:** reuse the canonical workflows.
- **Colour semantics:**
  - Navy/blue: navigation and actions.
  - Light blue: folders and neutral cards.
  - Soft yellow: Business Action Items only.
  - Green: completed, paid, or available.
  - Amber: awaiting or due soon.
  - Red: blocking errors or STOP only.
  - Charcoal: body text.

### D1. Business tab instruction.docx
- **Sidebar:** Home | My Documents | Intake Forms | Messages | Appointments | Invoices & Payments | My Services | Log Out. My Profile is absent.
- **Sections:** Your Business Documents (name, type, service, date shared, size, status, actions), Upload Document, View Document, View All, Business Action Items, My Business Services, Helpful Resources, Upcoming Appointment, Need Help, Recent Activity, Quick Links.
- **View All:** expands a scrollable panel on the same page.
- **Business document filters:** Search plus Category/Service/Year/Status over one dataset. The client cannot change staff review statuses. The row menu is limited to view details, download, and replacement-request.
- **Business Action Items are generated automatically:**
  - A document request shows its due date and Upload Now / Continue Request. It clears when submitted or closed.
  - An unfinished intake form shows Complete / Continue on the same record.
  - A correction or replacement request gets a direct action.
  - Nothing outstanding shows "all caught up".
  - Staff never retype these items.
- **Helpful Resources:** each opens an internal detail page.
  - Business Startup Guide / Business Setup & Compliance
  - Record Keeping

## 3. Client sign-up and access

- **The mockups show self sign-up.** The public LVP website page (Client portal landing page) offers **Sign In** and **Create an Account**.   1. **Create Account:** Full Name, Email, Phone, Password + Confirm, **Account Type**, and acceptance of the Terms of Service / Privacy Policy.
  2. **Verify Email:** a 6-digit code to the email, Resend after a 45 s countdown, and Change Email Address.
  3. **Verify Phone:** a 6-digit SMS code to the masked number, Resend (0:45), and Change Phone Number.
  4. **Complete:** "Account Created!" with Go to Client Portal or Return to Homepage.
- **Account types:** **Individual** ("personal tax services") or **Business** ("business tax, bookkeeping and formation services"). These are chosen at sign-up. No mockup shows a combined individual-plus-business account. The Tax Returns doc does mention a client linked to multiple businesses.

## 5. Differences / conflicts

### Against the stated assumptions
1. **Portal URL `portal.firmivra.com/{firm-slug}`:** no URL appears in any mockup or doc. The portal is shown as reached from the **firm's own website** (the LVP nav item "Client Portal"), entirely LVP-branded. The product name is "FirmVora" or "FirmVRA" throughout, never "Firmivra". Decide whether the portal lives on a firm subdomain or custom domain, or on the slug path.
2. **Clients only join via the firm:** this **conflicts**. The mockups show public **self sign-up** ("Create a New Account… It only takes a few minutes"), open to "prospective clients", with no invite or firm code. The only firm-gating is per-feature: an upload needs an active service, and an intake form needs self-service enabled.

## 6. New items not in a typical tax portal spec

- **Upload gate with a STOP state:** a three-way decision pop-up (tax / business / no open service) that blocks all uploads without an active service. The portal is "not for safekeeping".
- **Data-driven action lists:** Business Action Items and Your Next Steps are derived automatically from open requests, unfinished forms, corrections, signatures, and invoices, and clear themselves.
- **Seven service-specific intake forms** with record reuse, a full state machine (Sent → In Progress → Submitted → Needs Correction → Under Review → Completed / Expired / Archived), per-form self-service toggles, versioned submissions, and "Completed only after firm acceptance".
- **Business-services hub:** bookkeeping, payroll, advisory, and formation status alongside tax, with Service Progress bars.
- **Firm-curated educational resource library:** Business Startup Guide (entity types), Record Keeping (retention table), Payroll Resources (deadlines, forms, employee vs contractor), Tax Deductions (12 categories), and an External Links directory that is data-driven with per-tenant visibility, a safe new-tab policy, and click tracking.
- **Tax Tip card** with firm-managed content.
- **Client personal Notes with reminders:** a private notepad, separate from messages and staff notes.
- **Message filters by direction** (from the firm / I sent), plus Mark unread, cross-device read sync, and a live unread badge.
- **Recurring-service policy text** shown inline (14-day notice, 60-day post-cancel access, 90-day reactivation, call for copies after 60 days), configured centrally.
- **Two separate cancellations:** portal-account cancellation is distinct from recurring-service cancellation, and records are kept.
- **Locked Full Name and DOB** with a staff-approved **Request Name Change** workflow.
- **Additional Information fields:** "How did you hear about us?" referral source and preferred contact method (Email / SMS / Phone / Portal).
- **Firm Uploaded vs My Uploaded documents** as filtered views of one repository, with replacement versioning and malware scanning.
- **Tax Returns category cards** (Quarterly / Annual Personal / Annual Business) and a conditional **Tax Return Payment** card.
- **Five invoice states:** All / Due / Paid / **Upcoming** / Canceled, with async ACH pending handling and webhook idempotency.
- **Appointment card with staff name and meeting method** (Zoom), plus a details modal and in-place reschedule.
- **Account type at sign-up** (Individual vs Business), with phone verification by SMS during sign-up.
- **Every page needs pages that are not specified:** Appointments, My Services, My Settings, a notification centre (bell), and the avatar menu are referenced but have neither a mockup nor a doc.
