### Tax Planning (step 8)

1. **Estimates are estimates.** Every estimate carries its tax year, when it was calculated and its basis; an unavailable one is `null` with a reason, never invented; an override needs the permission and a reason and keeps the model's figure beside it. The disclaimer text is `TAX_PLANNING_DISCLAIMER` and comes with plans, opportunities and calculator results.
2. **Opportunities are not recommendations.** Reading one never changes it; every disposition is an explicit action (`POST .../actions`), audited with its reason; `MARK_NOT_APPLICABLE` and `DISMISS` need a reason and may suppress re-screening until a date; nothing implements a strategy or changes a tax return. Duplicates (one open opportunity per client, library strategy and year) are 409 `DUPLICATE_OPPORTUNITY`.
3. **Versions.** Finalizing and sending keep an insert-only snapshot (`tax_plan_versions`): strategy names, priorities, estimates, overrides, assumptions, the inputs and the age of the data. Later edits never rewrite what was delivered. Only the client notes are sent, never the internal ones.
4. **Overlapping savings** are not double-counted: the plan total is the model's, and `overlapNotes` says what was applied. The overlap rules are the API's to define and document.
5. **Roles and reach.** Owner and Admin reach every client; Staff only their assigned clients (404 otherwise). The grid's capabilities decide Create/Edit plans, Review opportunities, Manage strategies, Override estimates, Send plans and resources, View financial inputs (the plan's `inputs` and an opportunity's `clientDataUsed` are `null` without it) and Planning Admin (the screening run).
6. **Schema gaps (no schema change made):**
   - **Resources** have no table (guides, checklists, videos, client-facing flag, add and archive). The contract has read and send only; the first API serves an approved catalog from code (`PlanningResourceKey`). Add and archive need a table.
   - **Custom milestones** on the Tax Calendar have no table either. The calendar reads tax deadlines (Compliance & Deadlines) and has no create route.
   - **Planning reports** use the Reports module; `ReportCategory` has no planning value (ADVISORY exists), so the planning reports share TAX or ADVISORY until a value is added.
   - **Tax tables** for the calculator and the screening rules live in code, per tax year.
7. **Documents** are uploaded in the Documents module for the plan's client (`document_id`), then linked with `POST .../plans/:id/documents`; only the client's own documents can be linked (409 `DOCUMENT_NOT_ALLOWED`).
8. **Opportunity navigation**: `GET .../opportunities/:id` takes the queue's filters and returns `previousId` and `nextId`, so Previous and Next keep the review state.
9. **No mock** yet: the client is real-API only in `apps/web/src/lib/api.ts`. A mock is a frontend task.

### Tax Planning API (step 8b)

10. **Permissions.** Owner and Admin do everything. Staff create, edit, review, send and see the figures of their assigned clients (404 for any other), but cannot manage the library, override an estimate or run the screening. The grid roles follow their `TAX_PLANNING` row: Create (create plans), Edit (change, review, see the figures), Approve (override, send, enter an adjustment), Manage (library, screening, duplicate plans). The route guard maps the method to the capability first (POST needs Create), so a role with Edit but not Create cannot take an opportunity action. The grid's own defaults give a Tax Preparer View only.
11. **Estimates.** Only strategies the model covers get a figure: a library strategy that lists `retirement_contributions` or `hsa_contributions` in its required inputs is priced as the room left under the year's limit (401(k) $23,500 in 2025 and $24,500 in 2026; HSA self-only $4,300 and $4,400), taxed at the plan year's federal bands on the plan's taxable income (or AGI less the standard deduction). Anything else is `null` with `unavailableReason` ("No estimate model covers this strategy..."); an authorized person can enter one as an override. Nothing is invented: a missing figure, filing status or table year is unavailable with the reason. The model's figure, its data year and when it was calculated are kept on the row; "recalculate" refreshes them and leaves earlier versions alone. **The tax tables in `tax-tables.ts` (2025 and 2026 federal brackets, standard deductions, limits) were typed from the published figures and need a check by tax staff before launch.**
12. **Overlap.** The plan total is the sum of the selected, not-declined strategies' figures (override first), except that retirement strategies share one limit: only the largest counts, and `overlapNotes` says which. Other categories add up.
13. **Calculator.** Federal ordinary income tax only (no self-employment tax, QBI, AMT, state tax or capital gains rates: a capital gain makes the result unavailable). Limits and phase-outs are not checked. The result is always recomputed on the server; a scenario saved to a plan is a snapshot (the database refuses a change). Calculator and scenarios need "View Financial Inputs" (403 `FINANCIAL_INPUTS_RESTRICTED`).
14. **Screening.** Two documented rules (`retirement-room`, `hsa-room`) read the figures of the client's plan for the tax year and fire for each active library strategy that requires the figure. A run covers one client or up to 500 clients (`queued: true` when more remain; there is no background worker yet, so run again by client). It creates `NEW` opportunities (source SYSTEM), refreshes a `NEW` one when its figures changed, and leaves alone everything a person reviewed, anything Implemented or Closed, and a Not Applicable or Dismissed one before its suppression date (or for good without one). After the date a new opportunity is created.
15. **Opportunity actions.** `START_REVIEW` (NEW only); `ASSIGN`, `SCHEDULE_DISCUSSION`, `SET_DOCUMENT` (a document marked missing moves an UNDER_REVIEW, ASSIGNED or DISCUSSION_SCHEDULED one to NEEDS_DOCUMENTS; all received moves it back); `ADD_TO_PLAN` (the plan given, else the client's newest live plan of the year, else a new draft named "<year> Tax Plan"; the strategy is added once); `MARK_NOT_APPLICABLE` / `DISMISS` (reason; optional suppression date); `MARK_IMPLEMENTED` (not from NEW; records the date, touches no plan or return); `CLOSE` (not from NEW or a disposition); `REOPEN` (only Not Applicable or Dismissed; Closed is final); `ADD_NOTE`. Each is audited with the action and the statuses, never the reason or note. A `NOTE` is kept in the event's `details`.
16. **Versions and sending.** Finalize needs a selected strategy and no stale figure (older than 365 days; the period is a constant, no firm setting yet). Send needs a FINALIZED version and no change since (a change after it is 409 `PLAN_NOT_READY`: finalize again). Sending creates a portal message thread for the client (subject, client notes, each selected strategy with its estimate or "not available", the total and the disclaimer; never the internal notes) through the Messages module, which notifies the client by its own link-only email; the `channel` is recorded in the audit row, and both channels deliver the same message. The SENT version keeps what was sent. A unavailable estimate does not block finalizing: it is kept and shown as unavailable.
17. **Calendar and overview.** Tax deadlines are the firm's Compliance & Deadlines rows of types TAX_FILING, ESTIMATED_TAX, ELECTION, SALES_TAX and STATE_FILING that are not done, grouped by name and day (the id is the first row's); planning milestones come from the plans' target and discussion dates (`<planId>:target` / `:discussion`). KPI changes: clients "vs last quarter" counts the plans created over 90 days ago; appointments compare the next 30 days with the last 30. The other two KPIs have no comparison. Lists that group in memory (Clients tab, overview) read at most 5,000 rows.
18. **Resources.** Six entries in `catalog.ts` (one staff-only reference). Sending one creates a portal message with its summary; a staff-only one is 400. No table yet (see 6).
19. **Not built / needs others.** No schema change was made. **Infra: none.** Open follow-ups: a firm setting for the stale-figure period; resources add and archive (table); custom calendar milestones (table); background screening; an inbound client-facing view of the sent plan (the client reads the message); library strategies are created by the firm (no starter set); the estimator covers retirement and HSA strategies only; `CALCULATION_UNAVAILABLE` (503) is never returned because the calculation is local.
