### Business Advisory (step 9)

1. **Plan documents are validated here.** `advisory_plans.profile`, `.assessment` and `.strategy` are JSON the API validates with the zod schemas `AdvisoryPlanProfile`, `AdvisoryPlanAssessment` and `AdvisoryPlanStrategy` (strict, every field optional so a draft saves at any step). The EIN is not in the profile: only its last 4 digits, masked, on the client detail and only for those who may see it. A change merges the three documents at their top-level keys.
2. **Services come from Services & Pricing** (`GET /advisory/services`): a firm enables, renames, prices and packages them without code. One Primary workstream per client (making another primary demotes the old one); a declined service stays as DECLINED. Services that are regulated specialist needs are `referralOnly` and tracked as referral or project steps.
3. **The Business Health Score is explainable**: the API calculates it from the firm's formula (`advisory_settings.health_formula`, factor weights summing to 100), stores the factors and time on the advisory client, and the contract returns the factors, each factor's value (null when its data is missing, and left out of the score) and whether the data is stale. No opaque score.
4. **Versions.** Finalizing keeps an immutable `advisory_plan_versions` row with the profile, assessment, strategy, goals, recommendations, workstreams and financial assumptions and the source-data date; sending records who, when and how (`PORTAL`, `EMAIL`, `DOWNLOAD`). Only client-visible recommendations and action items are in what is sent.
5. **Financials** are insert-only snapshots with source and as-of date; a correction is a new row. A manual override of figures that came from a source needs a reason and the permission (403 `OVERRIDE_NOT_ALLOWED`), keeps the earlier row, and is audited. Net profit must equal revenue minus expenses when all three are given (409 `INCONSISTENT_FIGURES`). Figures are `null` without "View financial analysis".
6. **Statuses** follow Octavia's lists: plan (Draft, In Review, Finalized, Sent, In Progress, Completed, Archived; Finalized and Sent only through their routes), goal (Not Started to Cancelled; Overdue is also worked out when read from a past target date), recommendation and service.
7. **Roles and reach.** Owner and Admin reach every advisory client; Staff only the clients assigned to them (404 otherwise); the grid's capabilities decide plans, goals, recommendations, financial analysis, overrides, services, sending and Advisory Admin (settings).
8. **Schema gaps (no schema change made):**
   - **Resource library** has no table (formation, EIN, branding, website, banking, funding, planning, research, financial, growth, education; client-facing flag). The contract has read and send only; the first API serves an approved catalog from code (`AdvisoryResourceKey`). Add and archive need a table.
   - **Notes, documents, meetings and the client-portal tab** are not new tables: they use the client's BUSINESS_DEVELOPMENT engagement (notes, documents, appointments tagged Business Advisory) and the `clientVisible` flags on recommendations and action items. `GET /advisory/meetings` reads the appointments; scheduling is in Appointments.
   - **Advisory reports** use the Reports module; `ReportCategory` has `ADVISORY`.
   - **Document types** for advisory documents (business plan, financials, funding, formation, branding, website deliverables, banking checklist, licenses) are the Documents module's categories; none is added here.
9. **No mock** yet: the client is real-API only in `apps/web/src/lib/api.ts`. A mock is a frontend task.

### Business Advisory API (step 9b)

10. **Permissions.** Owner and Admin do everything. Staff create and change plans, goals, recommendations, action items and services, see the figures and send, for their assigned clients only (404 for any other); they cannot override figures or open the settings. The grid roles follow their `BUSINESS_ADVISORY` row: Create (plans, services, goals), Edit (change, recommendations, see the figures), Approve (override, send), Manage (settings, and all of those). The route guard maps the method to the capability first (POST needs Create). Reading the figures without the permission is 403 `FINANCIALS_RESTRICTED`; the score itself (0 to 100 and its factors) is not a figure and shows to all.
11. **Services.** The firm's advisory services are the active Services & Pricing entries of kind Business Development, with the packages that include them. `referralOnly` is always false: nothing in the catalog marks a regulated need yet (needs a flag on the service). Adding a client takes one primary service (status Accepted) and any additional ones (Interested); a client in the program once, archived included; the Business Development engagement of the client, when it has one, is linked (the API creates none).
12. **Business Health Score.** Seven factors in code (`health.ts`): revenue trend, profit margin, cash flow, debt load, goal progress, action items done and revenue against budget, each valued 0 to 100 with a plain explanation. The firm's formula (settings; weights add up to 100; default in code) picks and weights them. A factor with no data has no value and is left out; the rest are re-weighted; no data at all is no score. The score is stored with the factors it was made from and is worked out again when financial figures, goals or action items change (not when the formula changes: new calculations only). The client's version (etag) does not move when the score does. Stale = the newest figures are older than the firm's days, or there are none.
13. **Plans.** `profile`, `assessment` and `strategy` are validated with the contract's strict schemas and merged at their top-level keys on a change (a key sent as null is removed). Statuses: Draft and In Review move to each other; In Progress and Completed need a finalized plan; Finalized and Sent only by their routes; Archived by archive (restore goes back to Sent, Finalized or Draft). Finalize needs the business name, a strategy summary or an area, at least one goal on the plan and fresh financial figures (409 `PLAN_NOT_READY` lists what is missing); a stale or missing figure blocks it. A version keeps the content, the goals and recommendations as they were, the workstreams and the latest figure of each period. Send needs a version and the plan's own content unchanged since it (compared by content, not by time) unless an older version is named; a change after finalizing means finalize again. A version's delivery is set once (database rule), so a second delivery of a delivered version is kept as a new version with the same content and the new delivery on it. Portal and Email create a portal message for the client through Messages (plan name, summary, goals, client-visible recommendations and open steps, never a figure or an internal note; the client is told by Messages' own link-only email); Download only records the delivery. `deliveredChannel` is `portal`, `email` or `download`.
14. **Financials.** Insert-only. Net profit is worked out from revenue and expenses; a given one that differs is 409 `INCONSISTENT_FIGURES`. A MANUAL or UPLOADED row for a client and period that already has a BOOKKEEPING, PAYROLL or BANK row is an override: it needs the permission (403 `OVERRIDE_NOT_ALLOWED`) and `override.reason` (400); an `override` where there is nothing to replace is 400. The list shows the newest row of each client and period (all with `includeSuperseded`). Totals are over every filtered row; the budget variance is over rows with both a revenue and a budget.
15. **Goals.** `area` and a recommendation's `category` are kept as lower-case words with underscores (the columns' rule): "Revenue Growth" is `revenue_growth`; the filter takes either. A status and its progress go together (Completed is 100, Not Started is 0; a Completed goal is changed by status first). An open goal past its target date is shown as OVERDUE and found by that status. A plan, service, goal or milestone named for a client must be that client's (409 `NOT_THIS_CLIENT`; one the caller cannot reach, or another firm's, is 404). An archived advisory client, plan or goal cannot be changed.
16. **Action items.** A staff item with no one named is the author's own. A change to another kind clears the person; a change back to staff must name one. The assignee is an active member (a Staff member only when the client is theirs).
17. **Meetings** are the firm's scheduled and past appointments of advisory clients that are tagged by the Business Development service (their own or their engagement's); scheduling stays in Appointments. A video meeting with a link is `joinUrl`, anything else is `location`.
18. **Activity** is the audit trail of the client: every advisory action carries `advisoryClientId` in its audit row (ids, statuses and field names only), and the feed turns the action into a sentence; reads are left out.
19. **Not built / needs others.** No schema or infra change. Open follow-ups: a flag on services for referral-only needs; resources add and archive (table); meetings are read-only; resources are an approved list in code; the EIN shows as `XX-XXX<last 4>` to Owner, Admin and anyone who may see the figures; KPI changes compare 90 days ago (clients, plans) and the last 30 days (meetings), the others have none.
